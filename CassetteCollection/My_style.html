"My style" scale (0-4)

A scale to categorize cassette-tapes by how similar the music is to the music I like.

0 No Not at all music that I like; none of the tracks are enjoyable
1 Not really Most of it I do not like; there may be a few that are slightly good, but the rest is not
2 Kind of between 1 & 3 No strong opinions either way; the music isn't bad or unenjoyable, but I wouldn't want to obtain more of it solely because it's good
3 Pretty much The music is quite good; It would be nice obtain more of it
4 Absolutely This is exactly what I like; some of my favorite songs are probably on it

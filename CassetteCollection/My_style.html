"My style" scale (0-4)

A scale to categorize cassette-tapes by how similar the music is to the music I like.

Keep in mind that "not my style" does NOT mean "bad". Music can & usually is good, even if it is not my style.

0 No Not my style at all
1 Not really Most of it is not my style; there may be a few that are slightly my style, but the rest is not
2 No opinions No strong preference either way; the music is not my style, but it is also not not my style
3 Pretty much The music is quite my style; It would be nice obtain more of it
4 Absolutely This is exactly my style; some of my favorite songs are probably on it

Cassette 2026/08/02 #1 "1989 dec 13"

Bought on 2 August 2026, the same day as my cassette player. In total, 9 cassettes were bought that day, 1 of which came with the cassette player, the other 8 were together in a box. One of those 8 I have already overwritten, so that leaves 8 cassettes.

My best guess as to what the sticker on its A-side says is 13 December 1989 (1989 dec 13), Taylor Swift's date of birth. One big problem, however, is that what is supposed to be the E in "dec" looks nothing like E, but more like O. Another problem is that, as far as I know, this cassette contains nothing from Taylor Swift. Perhaps this cassette was instead recorded on that date, but that would be quite a coincidence.

Climates of my Minecraft worlds

Tables displaying the climates of my Minecraft worlds, similarly to weatherboxes on Wikipedia. Data comes from weather-stations at my home in each world, saved each in seperate spreadsheets.

The data are:

  1. Maximum Temperature: Warmest temperature observed on any given day.
  2. Minimum Temperature: Coldest temperature observed on any given day.
  3. Maximum Dewpoint: Warmest dewpoint observed on any given day. Minimum Dewpoint is not saved since dewpoint is not as important as temperature, & I already have enough data to deal with anyway.
  4. Humidity: Humidity observed in the morning.
  5. Moisture: Soil-moisture observed in the morning.
  6. Tornadoes: Amount of tornadoes observed on any given day.
  7. Risk: Maximum SPC risk-category observed on any given day.
  8. Snow: Average height of snow in the area surrounding the weather-station in the morning, eyeballed.
  9. Leaf color: Color of leaves on trees in the area in the morning, eyeballed.

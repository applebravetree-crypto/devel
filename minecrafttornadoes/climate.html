Climates of my Minecraft worlds

Tables displaying the climates of my Minecraft worlds, similarly to weatherboxes on Wikipedia. Data comes from weather-stations at my home in each world, saved each in seperate spreadsheets.

The data are:

  1. T maximum temperature: warmest temperature observed on any given day.
  2. T minimum temperature: coldest temperature observed on any given day.
  3. D maximum dewpoint: warmest dewpoint observed on any given day. Minimum Dewpoint is not saved since dewpoint is not as important as temperature, & I already have enough data to deal with anyway.
  4. H humidity: humidity observed in the morning.
  5. M moisture: soil-moisture observed in the morning.
  6. T tornadoes: amount of tornadoes observed on any given day.
  7. R risk: maximum SPC risk-category observed on any given day.
  8. S snow: average height of snow in the area surrounding the weather-station in the morning, eyeballed.
  9. L leaf color: color of leaves on trees in the area in the morning, eyeballed.

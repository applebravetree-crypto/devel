Rating guide for tornadoes of ProtoManly's Weather mod

Without changes to block strengths.

This table uses the Enhanced Fujita scale (+ EF6) for rating;

  • EF0: <=80 km/h
  • EF1: 100 km/h
  • EF2: 120 km/h
  • EF3: 140 - 160 km/h
  • EF4: 180 - 200 km/h
  • EF5: 220 - 260 km/h
  • EF6: >=280 km/h

When in doubt, choose lowest rating.

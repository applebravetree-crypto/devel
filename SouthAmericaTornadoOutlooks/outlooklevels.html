Outlook levels & what they mean

As of 2026/10/31, the GFS model, & sometimes the ECMWF IFS 0.1° together, are used to draw & issue outlooks. Models are accessed through WeatherWise.

Name Abbreviation Label on maps Appearance on maps Short description Detailed description
Tormentas TRM T Gris No tornadic environment Thunderstorms are expected to occur, but no tornadic environment is possible. No tornadoes are expected to occur.
Minúscula MSC 1 Verde Tornado might be possible There is a possibility of a tornadic environment, indicating a small possibility of a tornado occurring.
Moderada MDD 2 Amarilla Tornado possible or likely A tornadic environment is expected, meaning a tornado or 2 are possible
Mayor MYR 4 Rosa Tornadoes expected Major tornadic environment is expected. Tornadoes are very likely, or expected to occur. There is a significant possibility of multiple tornadoes occurring.
